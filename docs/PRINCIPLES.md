# Principles

1. **People before engagement.** Optimize for useful real-world participation, not screen time, streaks, or compulsive use.
2. **Privacy by default.** Do not collect personal data unless a clear user benefit requires it. Explain collection in plain language and provide meaningful opt-out and deletion.
3. **Accessible participation.** Make suggestions adaptable by time, mobility, cost, language, sensory needs, and remote access. Do not assume one life circumstance.
4. **Transparent guidance.** Make it clear what is a suggestion, what is known, and what is uncertain. Never present a prototype as a professional or emergency service.
5. **Human agency.** Users choose, change, or ignore suggestions. Avoid ranking people's worth, diagnosing them, or making consequential decisions about them.
6. **Evidence of impact.** Measure outcomes with consent and proportionate methods. Publish limitations, listen to affected communities, and stop features that cause harm.
7. **Community stewardship.** Curate local opportunities with the people who organize and participate in them; provide ways to report inaccurate or unsafe listings.
